# Opportunity Solution Tree

> **Status:** Hypothesis-only. Zero interview evidence has been collected; nodes must not be treated as validated.

## Desired outcome

Operators consistently begin and complete their highest-value daily follow-up from one evidence-backed briefing.

## Hypothesized opportunities

- **I cannot quickly tell what deserves attention now.**
  - I must inspect calls, conversations, appointments, campaigns, and CRM separately.
  - I cannot compare urgency, value, and compliance risk consistently.
- **I do not trust a recommendation without source evidence.**
  - Data may be stale, linked to the wrong contact, or omit consent state.
  - I need a rationale and reversible disposition.
- **I lose work when ownership or deferral is implicit.**
  - There is no shared queue or general task lifecycle.

## Candidate solutions—not commitments

1. Concierge-generated daily briefing with source links.
2. Read-only Today Queue using deterministic ranking.
3. Evidence-backed AI ranking in shadow mode.
4. Specialist-page improvements instead of a unified queue.

## Active experiment

Test solution 1 against the highest-risk assumption: operators will repeatedly change real work behavior when given one ranked, evidence-backed briefing.
