# Experiment: Unified Revenue Inbox Concierge + Shadow Mode

> **Status:** Prepared, not launched  
> **Archetype:** Concierge with read-only shadow recommendations

## Assumption

Operators repeatedly change real work behavior when given one ranked, evidence-backed briefing.

## Entry conditions

A named owner, approved segment, 5–8 consented participants across at least two profiles, isolated workspace access, frozen metrics, retention/deletion dates, and a two-person source/entity check.

## Protocol

1. Observe at least 3 baseline business days.
2. For 10 business days, prepare 5–15 read-only items per participant from existing calls, conversations, contacts, appointments, campaigns, compliance state, and configuration gaps.
3. Timestamp the ranking and evidence before the participant responds.
4. Capture act, complete, defer, dismiss, reassign, or incorrect plus outcome/time.
5. Score shadow recommendations separately; never execute or present them as facts.

## Pre-stated success criteria

All safety/data thresholds and at least four of five behavior/value thresholds in [`PRD-003`](../../requirements/backlog/prd-003-unified-revenue-inbox/prd-003-unified-revenue-inbox-index.md) must pass.

## Stop rules

Stop immediately for cross-workspace/entity exposure, unauthorized action, consent withdrawal not honored, uncontained PII disclosure, or evidence that continued participation creates material customer risk.

## Results

Not run. Complete the [readout template](../pass-pivot-stop-readout-template.md) without changing thresholds after seeing results.
