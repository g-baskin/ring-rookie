# Recruitment Screener

> **Status:** Template; recruitment not started

## Entry requirements

- Assign a named accountable discovery owner.
- Obtain security/compliance approval for the read-only data workflow.
- Recruit 5–8 active operators across at least two profiles, such as owner/operator and sales/support operator.
- Include both frequent and occasional Ring Rookie users; do not recruit only champions.

## Questions

1. What role do you play in lead follow-up, calling, messaging, booking, or campaign operations?
2. During the last five workdays, how many days did you use Ring Rookie?
3. Which surfaces did you use: calls, conversations, CRM, appointments, campaigns, agents, compliance, or integrations?
4. Describe the last time an important follow-up was delayed or missed.
5. Who else shares or takes over your work?
6. Are you willing to be observed during start-of-day planning and two queue-working sessions?
7. Separately choose whether to consent to notes, recording, transcription, and read-only production-data access.

## Exclusions

Exclude participants without recent relevant work, anyone unable to provide informed consent, and workspaces that cannot be isolated safely. Record profile, inclusion rationale, consent choices, and attrition using opaque participant IDs.
