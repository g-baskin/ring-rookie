# JTBD Interview and Observation Guide: Unified Revenue Inbox

> **Status:** Prepared, unexecuted | **Length:** 25–30 minutes

## Consent and safety

Confirm consent choices, workspace authorization, recording status, retention date, and the participant's right to stop. Do not copy raw names, phone numbers, emails, transcripts, or message bodies into notes.

## Five acts

1. **Context:** “Tell me about your role and how daily lead follow-up fits into it.”
2. **Big picture:** “Walk me through how you decide what needs attention on a normal day.”
3. **Recent story:** “Think about the last time an important follow-up was delayed, missed, or unexpectedly urgent. Walk me through exactly what happened.” Probe push, pull, anxiety, and habit without proposing the queue.
4. **Mental model:** “What evidence tells you an item is urgent, valuable, safe, and yours to handle?”
5. **Close:** “What did we miss about how you choose and complete daily work?”

## Observation prompts

Observe start-of-day planning and two work sessions. Record surfaces opened, sequence, search/filter behavior, time to first meaningful action, context switches, workarounds, evidence checked, deferrals, handoffs, and compliance uncertainty. Separate observation, direct quote, researcher inference, and shadow recommendation.

## Debrief

Immediately identify 1–3 candidate opportunity statements in customer language. Update the OST only with dated evidence; do not turn feature requests directly into opportunity nodes.
